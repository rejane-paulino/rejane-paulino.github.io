## GitHub Repositories

### S2/3Aqua (Sentinel-2/3 Synthetic Aquatic Reflectance Bands)<img src="/images/s23aqua.svg" width="130" align="right" />
The S2/3Aqua is a proto-algorithm that integrates spatial and spectral domains from the Sentinel-2 MSI and Sentinel-3 OLCI images using multivariate regressor models. The S2/3Aqua combines these two sensors to create a new set of synthetic multi-band products that preserve each sensor’s unique characteristics... [Link](https://github.com/rejane-paulino/s23aqua)

### AerOC Scan<img src="/images/aerocscan.png" width="130" align="right" />
AerOC Scan is a Python package designed to download, filter, and correct AERONET-OC (Aerosol Robotic Network-Ocean Color) data. AERONET-OC comprises a global network of autonomous above-water radiometers located on fixed platforms in ocean, coastal, and inland waters... [Link](https://github.com/rejane-paulino/aerocscan)

### L-CONNECT (river-Lake CONNECTivity) <img src="/images/lconnect.svg" width="130" align="right" />
L-CONNECT framework combines machine learning algorithms and spectral similarity features to predict water surface connectivity between floodplain lakes and their main river. The assumption is that the spectral similarity between river and lake waters is a good proxy for hydrological connectivity... [Link](https://github.com/rejane-paulino/lconnect)

#
### (AWP - Inland Water) Adaptative Window by Proportion applied to Inland Water <img src="/images/awpinlandwater.svg" width="130" align="right" />
AWP-Inland Water is a proto-algorithm that has been developed to address the reduction of adjacency effect on satellite-imagery applied to small waterbodies. The key principle behind AWP-Inland Water lies in its ability to adapt window-ranges according to local conditions across the waterbodies... [Link](https://github.com/rejane-paulino/awp-inlandwater)
