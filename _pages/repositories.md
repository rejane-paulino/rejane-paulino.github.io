## [S2/3Aqua (Sentinel-2/3 Synthetic Aquatic Reflectance Bands)](https://github.com/rejane-paulino/s23aqua)<img src="/images/s23aqua.svg" width="150" align="right" />

The S2/3Aqua is a proto-algorithm that integrates spatial and spectral domains from the Sentinel-2 MSI and Sentinel-3 OLCI images using multivariate regressor models. The S2/3Aqua combines these two sensors to create a new set of synthetic multi-band products that preserve each sensor’s unique characteristics...
